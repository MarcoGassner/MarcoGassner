Hi, my name is Marco.

I study digital mental health interventions to understand how we can improve prevention and treatment outcomes using randomized trials and usage data. Currently I am working on the implementation of a blended psychoeducational approach for families with a history of forced displacement. I investigate how culturally sensitive and developmentally tailored systems can provide early-stage support to prevent symptom escalation and ensure equitable access for underserved groups.

Currently I am a Visiting Researcher at the Division of Interdisciplinary Brain Sciences within the Department of Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences at Stanford.

When I’m not working, you’ll likely find me running, swimming, or climbing outdoors. 🪨🌊🏞️

I’m always happy to connect!

InterAPPtion

InterAPPtion

The aim of the planned study is to investigate the effectiveness of the mobile application InterAPPtion, both as a standalone intervention and in combination with the parent training program Parents’ College. The study examines whether InterAPPtion increases adherence, promotes parents’ knowledge acquisition, and supports child development through targeted everyday guidance. In addition, it assesses whether the combined approach reduces the overall impairment of the children.
