Hi, my name is Marco.

I study digital mental health interventions to understand, how we can improve prevention and treatment outcomes, using randomized trials and usage data. Currently I am working on the implentation of a blended psychoeducational approach for families with a history of forced displacement. I investigate how culturally sensitive and developmentally tailored systems can provide early-stage support to prevent symptom escalation and ensure equitable access for underserved groups. Thankfully, I get to explore all of this alongside curious and inspiring people, at the Technical University of Munich, at the Chair of Social Pediatrics.

Currently I am a Visiting Researcher at the Division of Interdisciplinary Brain Sciences within the Department of Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences at Stanford.

When I’m not working, you’ll likely find me running, swimming, or climbing outdoors. 🪨🌊🏞️

Project Cluster

InterAPPtion

The aim of the planned study is to investigate the effectiveness of the mobile application InterAPPtion, both as a standalone intervention and in combination with the parent training program Parents’ College. The study will examine whether InterAPPtion increases adherence, promotes parents’ knowledge acquisition, and supports child development through targeted everyday guidance. In addition, it will assess whether the combined approach reduces the overall impairment of the children. The primary focus is on a reduction in symptoms, lower stress levels, and more positive play, interaction, and learning behaviors. The app content is modularized based on the manual and prepared in a multimedia format. It is provided in eight languages (Arabic, Dari/Farsi, Turkish, Russian, Spanish, French, English, and German). In addition to psychoeducational videos and exercises, a community function enables participants to share homework within a closed course group. The target group is actively involved in the further development of the app through an interactive and participatory process. The app is currently in the development and testing phase. Once completed, it will be made available here.
