Hi, my name is Marco, and I care about the mental health of children and adolescents. I study digital mental health interventions to improve prevention and treatment outcomes, with a particular interest in accessibility, tailored support, and vulnerable populations.

My research focuses on children and adolescents at developmental risk and explores how digital and blended approaches can improve access to care, prevention, and treatment.

Visiting Research Scholar at Stanford in the Division of Interdisciplinary Brain Sciences, Department of Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences .

Selected Work

My work centers on child and adolescent mental health, especially in vulnerable populations.
