Hi, my name is Marco.

I study digital mental health interventions to improve prevention and treatment outcomes, using randomized trials and usage data. My work focuses on how care structures can be expanded, improved, and individually tailored to reduce or prevent symptom burden and am, particularly interested in learning processes in human–technology interaction. I also explore how interventions can be adapted across cultures and developmental stages, with a focus on child and adolescent populations and underrepresented groups.

Thankfully, I get to explore all of this alongside curious and thoughtful people, as a PhD candidate in Behavioral and Social Sciences at the Graduate School of Medicine and Health, Technical University of Munich, working with a wonderful team at the Chair of Social Pediatrics. Currently I am a visiting researcher at the Division of Interdisciplinary Brain Sciences within the Department of Psychiatry and Behavioral Sciences at Stanford. When I’m not working, you’ll likely find me running, swimming, or climbing outdoors.

I’m always happy to connect with people interested in similar or entirely different topics. Feel free to reach out: marco.gassner@tum.de OR mgassner@stanford.edu
